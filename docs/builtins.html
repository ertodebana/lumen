Chapter 8

Built-in Functions

Two functions are available everywhere without being declared.

FunctionArgumentsReturns
len(s)one stringThe number of characters in s.
clock()noneThe current time as seconds since 1 January 1970 (UTC), with a fractional part.

len

len accepts only strings.

To count the digits of a number, turn it into a string first:

clock

The absolute value of clock() is rarely interesting. The difference between two readings is: it tells you how long something took.

Built-ins are ordinary values

They can be stored, passed around and printed like any function. They can also be shadowed: a let len = ... of your own hides the built-in within that scope.
